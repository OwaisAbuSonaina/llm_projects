# Edward Donner: Innovating Talent Matching with AI

## About Edward Donner

Edward Donner is a passionate coder, AI enthusiast, and educator known for his deep expertise and infectious enthusiasm about large language models (LLMs). He is the co-founder and CTO of **Nebula.io**, an AI startup revolutionizing how recruiters source, understand, engage, and manage talent. With a background that includes founding a previous AI company, Untapt (acquired in 2021), and experience as a Managing Director at JPMorgan, Edward brings a unique blend of entrepreneurial spirit and technical mastery to the AI field.

---

## Nebula.io: Transforming Talent Acquisition with Generative AI

At Nebula.io, Edward and his team leverage Generative AI and machine learning to drastically improve the hiring process. Their patented AI model matches candidates with job roles based on deep understanding—outperforming traditional keyword searches by delivering matches with unmatched speed and accuracy.

- **Mission:** To empower individuals to discover and pursue their true potential by finding roles where they will be fulfilled and successful, inspired by the Japanese concept of *Ikigai*.
- **Vision:** Raise global human prosperity by addressing the widespread lack of engagement and inspiration in the workplace—77% of people report feeling uninspired at work.
- **Product:** A free-to-try talent matching service that simplifies recruitment while enhancing both candidate and employer experiences.
